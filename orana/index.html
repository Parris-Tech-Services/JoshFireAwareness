To ask at the brigade

Good questions
make a good start.

  1. Is my Member Induction officially recorded as complete?
  2. Are RIW and the Code of Conduct acknowledgement required now?
  3. What are BF/BFG versus F1/F2, and what is my actual training order?

  5. Who confirms what activities I can safely participate in?
  6. Which app, pager and contact arrangements does our brigade use?
  7. What non-operational or community roles would be useful?
  8. Who can mentor me as I learn?

04 / Safe preparation

A few things
worth knowing.

Public safety principles and contextual information, not an operational field manual.

Emergency

Fire emergency? Call 000.

For current incident information, use NSW RFS Fires Near Me or call the Bush Fire Information Line on 1800 679 737. Official emergency guidance ↗

Season and permits

Dates can change locally.

The statutory Bush Fire Danger Period is normally 1 October–31 March, but declarations can vary it. Check current local permits and Total Fire Bans before any burning. Check RFS permits ↗

Grass and harvest

Think about the farm landscape.

Grassfires, machinery, harvest conditions, wind and ignition prevention are especially relevant to Orana's rural environment. Harvest fires guide ↗

People first

Wellbeing is part of service.

The RFS offers peer support, chaplaincy, psychologists and a Member Assistance Program for volunteers, with eligible family support. Member support ↗
